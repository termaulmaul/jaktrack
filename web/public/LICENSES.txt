The timetable data come from the Mini Tokyo 3D dataset (pinned snapshot; About shows its commit and the date it was
obtained). Train positions on the map are calculated from these timetables. They are not reported by the trains,
except that while live data is in use, a train whose delay is reported live is moved by that delay.

4. Basemap tiles
==============================================================================

By default the basemap tiles come from OpenFreeMap (https://openfreemap.org): "OpenFreeMap © OpenMapTiles Data from
OpenStreetMap" (OpenMapTiles schema, https://www.openmaptiles.org/; data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL).

built from OpenStreetMap data (a Produced Work under the ODbL, OpenStreetMap attribution required) with Natural Earth
(public domain, https://www.naturalearthdata.com).

7. Ground heights: GSI elevation tiles
==============================================================================

Used only when the 3D depth data is built (npm run data:3d):
出典：国土地理院（地理院タイル 標高タイル DEM5A・DEM5B・DEM10B）を加工して作成
Ground heights: Geospatial Information Authority of Japan (GSI) elevation tiles, processed (DEM5A, DEM5B, DEM10B).
Used for the tunnel depths of the underground view (rail level below the street). https://maps.gsi.go.jp/development/ichiran.html
